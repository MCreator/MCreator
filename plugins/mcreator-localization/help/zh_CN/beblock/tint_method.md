着色方法应用基于生物群系的颜色。

- **白桦树叶** -- <font style="background: #80A755">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</font> 用途：minecraft:birch_leaves
- **默认树叶** -- <font style="background: #77AB2F">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</font> 用途：minecraft:acacia_leaves, minecraft:dark_oak_leaves, minecraft:jungle_leaves, minecraft:mangrove_leaves, minecraft:oak_leaves, minecraft:vines
- **干燥树叶** -- <font style="background: #A37546">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</font> 用途：minecraft:leaf_litter
- **常青树叶** -- <font style="background: #619961">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</font> 用途：minecraft:spruce_leaves
- **草** -- <font style="background: #92BC58">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</font> 用途：minecraft:fern, minecraft:grass_block (top), minecraft:large_fern, minecraft:pink_petals (stems), minecraft:reeds, minecraft:short_grass, minecraft:tall_grass, minecraft:wildflowers (stems)
- **water** -- <font style="background: #44AFF5">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</font> 用途：minecraft:cauldron (undyed water), minecraft:flowing_water, minecraft:water
